Why hart?

A hart is a mature red deer stag — the word English used for centuries before "stag" took over. In the Sherwood stories it's the beast the King reserved for himself and the foresters hunted anyway. The white one was the rare sighting, the omen, the thing you told people about for years afterwards.

That's the whole idea. Old word, new chain, and a name that belongs to the people who use it rather than the crown that claimed it.
